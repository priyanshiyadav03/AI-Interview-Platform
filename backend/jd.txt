# Job Description: Associate Python AI Engineer

## Role Overview
We are looking for a passionate Python AI Engineer to join our tech team. In this role, you will work on building intelligent backend applications, optimizing machine learning models, and designing data-driven AI systems.

## Key Responsibilities
* Write clean, maintainable, and efficient Python code for backend applications.
* Build and maintain scalable AI pipelines using Large Language Models (LLMs) and Prompt Engineering.
* Implement Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipelines for enterprise document search.
* Collaborate with data engineering teams to preprocess datasets and perform feature engineering.
* Integrate machine learning models with RESTful APIs and microservices.

## Required Skills & Technologies
* Programming: Strong proficiency in Python.
* Frameworks & Libraries: LangChain, PyTorch, NumPy, Pandas, Scikit-learn.
* Databases & Tools: PostgreSQL, Git, Node.js, and REST API.
* AI/ML Concepts: Fine-tuning, Embeddings, Statistics, and Vector Databases.

## Soft Skills
* Good communication skills and ability to present ideas clearly.
* Strong teamwork mentality and collaborative problem-solving skills.
* Adaptability and willingness to learn new technologies quickly.

## Qualifications & Experience
* Experience: 0–3 Years of relevant technical experience (Freshers with strong projects can also apply).
* Education: Bachelor's degree (B.Tech or BCA) in Computer Science, Data Science, or a related field.
* Project: Experience with at least one hands-on AI/ML or LLM-based academic project is highly preferred.
